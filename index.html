About

Our Story

Sharma Microwave and Washing Machine Repair is a trusted home appliance repair service specializing in microwave ovens and washing machines of all major brands. We provide fast, reliable, and affordable repair, installation, maintenance, and spare parts replacement services for residential and commercial customers.

With over a decade of experience, our skilled technicians are trained to handle all types of issues – from simple part replacements to complex electronic repairs. We use genuine spare parts and the latest diagnostic tools to ensure long-lasting results.

Our commitment to quality, transparency, and customer satisfaction has made us a preferred choice for appliance repair in Greater Noida and surrounding areas. We believe in building trust through honest service and exceptional workmanship.
